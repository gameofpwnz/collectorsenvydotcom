Open Data

Free, read-only feeds of what Collectors Envy publishes on this site, for community bots, spreadsheets and tools. Please credit collectorsenvy.com wherever you show it.

Rules of the road

  • Read-only and best effort. Fields can change, and a feed can be down.
  • Please cache responses for at least 5 minutes and do not poll faster. The feeds run on a free Google service with daily limits that everyone shares.
  • Prices are what members reported, not appraisals. Estimates are opinions.
  • Do not use the data to harass members or impersonate Collectors Envy.

Feeds

Previously sold

Items sold through Collectors Envy, newest first.

Returns { items: [...] }. Each item: description, price (text), date (text), seller, images (list of links).

Past estimates

Items the community has valued, newest first.

Returns { items: [...] }. Each item: description, price (text), date (text), image (link).

Museum

Verified pieces in the Collectors Envy museum.

Returns a list. Each piece: id, title, category, esport, year, steward, description, tags, media, verified.

Example

fetch('FEED_URL')
  .then(r => r.json())
  .then(data => console.log(data.items.slice(0, 3)));
