About Collectors Envy

A community for esports fans who buy, sell, trade and discover rare memorabilia and jerseys.

Who we are

Collectors Envy brings together collectors from all over the world who love esports history. Whether you're trying to complete a collection, trade with other fans or sell a valuable piece, we give passionate collectors a place to connect, find exclusive and hard-to-get items from the esports scene, and grow their collections together.

The community lives in our Discord server. This website is where we keep the things worth looking up later: estimates, auction results, event lists and the archive of what we've preserved.

What we do

  • Estimates: ask for a free price estimate on a jersey, controller or collectible, and browse past ones.
  • Auctions and Previously Sold: live community auctions and a record of what items have sold for.
  • Proxy: can't make an event? A member can attend and buy for you, and you can apply to be a proxy yourself.
  • Events: the esports and collector events our members attend.
  • Bounties: requests for hard-to-find pieces.
  • Reputation: vouches for sellers, buyers, traders and proxies, so you know who you're dealing with.
  • Museum, Archive and OpTic Scuf Archive: a digital record of esports memorabilia and the research behind it.
  • Merch: Collectors Envy gear.

Trading safely

Members vouch for each other after good deals, and those vouches are public on the Reputation page. Check someone's reputation before you trade, and vouch after a deal goes well.
