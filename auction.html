Request an Auction

Submit this form to auction an item in the Collectors Envy Discord. Your request goes to the admins, and once approved your photos and details become the live auction.
  • $5 per auction, or free if 5 people join the Discord with your invite code.
  • Auctions are for special items. Standard items go in the for-sale section.
  • Maximum length is 6 days. Make sure reserve and Buy It Now numbers are accurate.
  • Cancelling after a bid has been placed gets you banned from future auctions.
  • Shipping is negotiated with the winner and must happen within 5 days of the auction ending unless agreed otherwise.
